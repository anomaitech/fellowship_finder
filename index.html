Your Strengths

  • 7+ years surgical training (MD + 4yr general surgery + 3yr plastic surgery)
  • Microsurgery in Switzerland - international exposure highly valued
  • USMLE Step 1 passed - major hurdle cleared
  • Best match: Microsurgery & Research fellowships - leverage your Swiss training

Challenges to Address

  • F-2 visa cannot work/train - must change to J-1 or H-1B
  • ECFMG certification needed for ACGME programs (Step 1 + Step 2 CK + OET)
  • USMLE Step 2 CK - aim for 240+, ideally 250+
  • Some programs explicitly cannot sponsor visas (UCSF, UW, UTHSCSA)

Two-Track Strategy

Track 1 (Now - 2027): Apply to research fellowships + non-ACGME clinical fellowships. These are more IMG-accessible and build US connections.

Track 2 (2027-2028+): With ECFMG certification, apply to ACGME programs via SF Match/NRMP.

Visa Pathway & ECFMG Certification

F-2 Visa Cannot Be Used for Medical Training

Must change status to J-1 or H-1B before starting any fellowship.

J-1 Visa (Most Common)

  • Sponsored by ECFMG for clinical training
  • Standard for residency/fellowship
  • Drawback: 2-year home residency requirement
  • Can apply for J-1 waiver (3yr underserved area)
  • J-1 Research Scholar for research fellowships

H-1B Visa (Best Long-Term)

  • No 2-year home residency requirement
  • $100K+ filing fee (Sep 2025 change)
  • Fewer programs sponsor
  • Better for long-term US career

ECFMG Certification

  • Step 1: PASSED
  • Step 2 CK: In progress
  • Pathway: OET Medicine required
  • Required for ALL ACGME programs
  • ECFMG Pathways 2027

Visa Sponsorship by Program

  • J-1 Confirmed: Stanford, WashU (case-by-case)
  • No Visa: UCSF, UW, UTHSCSA, Texas Tech
  • IMG Accepted: Cleveland Clinic, MCW, MD Anderson
  • Always verify directly with each program
